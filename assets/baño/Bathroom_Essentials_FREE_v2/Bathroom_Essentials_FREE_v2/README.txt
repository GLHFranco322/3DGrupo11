Bathroom Essentials - free low-poly pack (10 models, 1,226 triangles)

These models come from Modern Urban Apartments v2, the full kit: https://anass-damiri.itch.io/modern-urban-apartments-kit-godot
Same palette, scale and style, so this free pack mixes with every Modern Urban v2 kit.

Folders: FBX, GLB (texture embedded), OBJ, Textures (256 px palette + emission).
Scale 1 unit = 1 m, origin on the ground, front -Y in Blender (+Z in glTF/Godot). Use nearest filtering on the palette.

Models: Toilet, Bathtub, Shower, Vanity, Washer, Bath_Mat, Towel_Rail, Wall_Tile_Bath_4m, Floor_Tile, Plant_Small
